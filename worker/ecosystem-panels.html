Your weekly loop
  1. Save or import opportunities and review the score breakdown.
  2. Shortlist a few gigs you can deliver well.
  3. Prepare a proposal grounded in your evidence and the actual brief.
  4. Move the opportunity into your pipeline and set a follow-up.
  5. Review wins, losses and recurring skill gaps.
